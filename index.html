RioRacer v2.0: Neo-Tokyo 3D

3D Cel-Shaded Anime Endless Runner

Welcome to RioRacer v2.0 (Neo-Tokyo 3D)! Take control of Rio in his iconic red Akira-inspired track jacket as he races down a futuristic 3-lane Neo-Tokyo highway in full cel-shaded 3D.

How to Play (v2.0 3D)

  • Switch Lanes (Left / Right): Press A / D, Left / Right Arrow Keys, Swipe Left / Right, or tap the ◄ / ► HUD buttons.
  • Jump & Double-Jump: Press SPACEBAR / W / Up Arrow, Tap / Swipe Up, or press the JUMP button. Press again mid-air for an anime somersault Double-Jump!
  • Golden Bone (Super Rio): Collect the floating 3D Golden Bone to equip Rio's Golden Hero Visor & Cape—smash straight through rivals for 10 seconds!
  • Clutch Near-Miss (+100): Graze past an obstacle at the last split-second to trigger a CLUTCH! +100 bonus!

Major Release Features

  • 🎌 Cel-Shaded 3D Anime Graphics: Real-time Three.js toon shading, manga ink outlines, and dynamic chase camera.
  • 🌃 Neo-Tokyo Highway: 3-lane cyber street with glowing Japanese signboards, Torii arches, and Hard/Expert atmosphere shifts.
  • 🏆 Global Leaderboard: Compete worldwide in real time via Firebase.
  • 🕹️ Classic 2D Preserved: Switch anytime to the original v1.9.20 2D side-scroller using the Play Classic 2D button on the start screen.

Meet the Real Rio 🐶

Inspired by a real fluffy dog's daily adventures—now elevated into a futuristic Japanese anime hero!
